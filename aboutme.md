About Me

Hey, I’m Michael!

I was born and raised in Lugano, Switzerland, and I’m passionate about many things, especially computer science, biology and design.

I earned my BSc in Biology from the University of Fribourg in 2023. Right after, I did an MSc in Bioinformatics and Computational Biology, a joint degree between the Universities of Fribourg and Bern, which I finished in September 2025.

I’d call myself a people person. To me, real connection with the people around me matters more than any performance metric, because people who get along well together achieve more and attract more.
